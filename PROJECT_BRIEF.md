# Dhow Cruise Dubai — Enhanced Product Brief

## Objective

Design and build an award-calibre, mobile-first Next.js marketing and booking website for a premium dinner dhow cruise operating from Dubai Marina. The project must work both as a believable commercial product and as a portfolio centrepiece capable of attracting tourism and hospitality companies.

## Brand direction

- Brand: **Alishba Cruises** (Alishba Dhow Cruise, est. 2022, operated under Collective Dynamic Holding) — two vessels: **Alishba Costa** (relaxed) and **Alishba Royale** (premium double-deck).
- Verified facts live in `lib/site.ts` (prices, sailing time, phone/WhatsApp, email, office). Update them there only.
- Brand tokens (sampled from alishbadhowcruise.com): teal `#0C728B`, burnt orange `#B0572C`, slate `#3A4463`, on deep night-teal `#071a22` and warm ivory `#fbf8f2`. Accessible tints: `#9E4C25` (orange text on light), `#E8935F` (orange text on dark), `#6FC0D3` (teal on dark).
- Type: EB Garamond (editorial headings, true italics for accents) + Inter (UI/body), self-hosted via Fontsource.
- Visual language: cinematic night photography of the real vessels, editorial typography, subtle glass surfaces and refined micro-interactions.
- Tone: warm, precise, confident and hospitable. Avoid inflated luxury clichés and unverified claims.

## Experience requirements

- A narrative homepage that moves from desire to detail to decision.
- Dedicated pages for cruises, onboard experience, brand story, contact and booking.
- A clear multi-step booking flow covering cruise, date, sailing time, guest count, contact details and special requests.
- Fully responsive navigation and layouts from small phones to wide desktops.
- Tasteful motion with reduced-motion support.
- Accessibility: semantic landmarks, keyboard-visible focus, labels, contrast, generous tap targets and meaningful image alternatives.
- SEO: unique metadata, canonical-ready structure, Open Graph data and JSON-LD for a tourism business/product.
- Strong performance defaults via Next Image, local assets and minimal client-side JavaScript.

## Frontend scope

- Home
- Cruises
- Experience
- About
- Contact
- Book
- Original hero and dining-deck artwork
- Reusable site header, footer, reveal animation and page hero components

## Deferred backend scope

- Live availability and inventory
- Price rules and promo codes
- Payment gateway
- Email/WhatsApp confirmations
- CRM/admin dashboard
- Booking persistence, cancellation and refund workflows
- Analytics/consent implementation

## Success criteria

The finished frontend should feel launch-ready, make the booking path obvious within seconds, remain polished on mobile, and demonstrate strong product thinking—not only surface decoration.
